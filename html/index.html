Registration Steps To Follow.

  1. Choose a Training Programme: Visit the ICT training centre and select the course you wish to study, such as Computer Applications, Web Design, Graphic Design, Programming, or Networking.
  2. Complete the Registration Form: Obtain and fill in the student registration form with your personal details, educational background, and contact information.
  3. Submit Required Documents: Provide the necessary documents, such as a copy of your National ID or birth certificate, passport-size photographs, and academic documents where applicable.
  4. Pay Registration Fees and Confirm Admission: Pay the required registration or tuition fees, receive your payment receipt, and collect your admission confirmation with information about the course start date and class schedule.

Yes Training Center

We are a dedicated ICT Training Center committed to empowering individuals with the
knowledge, skills, and practical experience needed to succeed in today's rapidly evolving
digital world. Our center was established with a vision of making quality information and
communication technology education accessible to everyone, regardless of their educational
background or level of experience. We believe that technology plays a vital role in personal
development, business growth, education, and national development. Therefore, we provide a
supportive and engaging learning environment where students, professionals, entrepreneurs, and
beginners can develop essential computer and digital skills. Our training programs are designed
to combine theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical sessions, ensuring that every learner
gains the confidence and ability to apply their skills in real-life situations.

At our ICT Training Center, we offer a wide range of courses, including basic computer applications,
Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Access), internet and email usage, graphic design,
web design, computer maintenance, networking, database management, and other essential digital
skills. We are committed to providing quality training through experienced instructors, practical
demonstrations, modern learning approaches, and continuous support to our learners. Our goal is
not only to teach people how to use computers but also to prepare them for employment opportuni
ties, entrepreneurship, innovation, and further studies in the technology sector. We value exce
llence, professionalism, teamwork, creativity, and lifelong learning. Through our commitment
to quality education and skills development, we aim to bridge the digital skills gap, promote
digital inclusion, and contribute to building a knowledgeable, skilled, and technologically
empowered community ready to embrace the opportunities of the digital age.
